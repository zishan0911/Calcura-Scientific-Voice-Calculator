Frequently Asked Questions

Quick answers to help you solve problems instantly.

How do I use voice input in Calcura?

Open Voice Input, allow microphone access when requested, press the microphone button and speak your mathematical expression.

Is Calcura free to use?

Calcura provides a Free Plan with basic calculation features. Additional features can be available through the Pro plan.

Can I save my calculations and formulas?

Yes. Use the Save option where available. Your saved calculations can be accessed from the Saved page and previous calculations can be viewed in History.

How do I upgrade to Pro?

Open the Upgrade to Pro page from the sidebar or the button in the top navigation.

Does Calcura work offline?

Offline availability depends on the feature and the current Calcura implementation.

How do I contact support?

Select a support topic in the Contact Support panel and send your message. You can also use the support options shown on this page.
